Runtime jars required alongside app.jar but intentionally excluded from the
shaded uber-jar (see pom.xml maven-shade-plugin <artifactSet> excludes):

  io.delta:delta-spark_2.12:3.2.0
  io.delta:delta-storage:3.2.0
  org.apache.hadoop:hadoop-azure:3.3.6
  org.apache.hadoop:hadoop-azure-datalake:3.3.6
  com.azure:azure-messaging-eventhubs:5.18.4

Resolve these into ${APP_HOME}/jars/ during image build with:
  mvn dependency:copy-dependencies -DincludeScope=runtime -DoutputDirectory=docker/libs

then add to Dockerfile:
  COPY docker/libs/*.jar ${APP_HOME}/jars/

and reference all jars on the classpath via --jars in spark-submit
(see k8s/spark-submit-command.sh) or bake them into $SPARK_HOME/jars/
so every driver/executor pod already has them without a spark-submit
--jars upload on every run.
